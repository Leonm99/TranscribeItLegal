2. What Information Do We Collect?

  • Account Data: Email addresses (via Firebase Authentication / Social Login) to manage your Pro subscription status.

3. How Do We Process Your Information?

We process your information to provide the following services:

  • Transcription: Audio/video files are encrypted in transit and sent to Groq, Inc. (USA) for speech-to-text processing. Files are deleted from Groq's systems immediately after transcription.
  • Summarization (Pro): Transcripts are sent to OpenAI, LLC (USA) for AI-powered summarization. Input content is NOT used to train OpenAI models.
  • Translation (Pro): Transcripts are sent to OpenAI, LLC (USA) for translation. Input content is NOT used to train OpenAI models.
  • Account Management: Firebase Authentication manages your account and subscription status.
  • Anonymous Analytics: Firebase Analytics records aggregate usage events without linking personal identifiers.

4. Detailed Analytics (Firebase)

We log specific events to understand how the app is used. No personal identifiers (like email or name) are linked to these events. You can disable this collection at any time in the App Settings > Usage Analysis.

  • General Interaction: App first open, session start, and screen views.
  • Feature Usage: Statistics on transcription starts (e.g., file count) and success (e.g., text length), as well as usage of AI features (Summarization, Translation).
  • Subscription Events: Interactions with the billing screen (e.g., clicking a price, subscription updates/cancellations via Google Play).
  • Performance & Stability: Startup time, API response times (HTTP status), and crash reports.

We rely on the following legal bases to process your personal information:

  • Performance of a Contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): To deliver the transcription, summarization, and translation services you requested.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): For optional analytics (which you can revoke via Settings at any time).
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): To diagnose technical issues, prevent fraud, and improve the application's functionality based on anonymous usage patterns.

7. Sharing Your Information

We share data strictly with the following third-party vendors under Data Processing Addendums (DPAs):

  • Groq, Inc. (USA): Speech-to-Text processing.
  • OpenAI, LLC (USA): Translation and summarization.
  • Google Ireland Limited (Ireland): Firebase — Authentication, Database, Cloud Functions, and anonymous usage analysis.
AI & Data Training Your uploaded content and generated transcripts are NOT used to train the AI models of our providers (Groq/OpenAI).

8. Data Retention

  • Uploaded Audio/Video Files: Deleted from Groq's active processing buffers immediately after transcription is complete. We do not retain copies.
  • Transcripts: Stored locally on your device in an encrypted Room database. If you use Google Drive backup (Pro), transcripts are included in your personal Google Drive backup — we have no access to that data.
  • Firebase Authentication Data: Retained until you delete your account.
  • Anonymous Analytics: Aggregated data is retained for up to 14 months in accordance with Firebase Analytics data retention settings.

9. Data Security

All data in transit to our service providers is encrypted using TLS 1.2+. Audio/video files are sent directly to Groq for processing. We do not encrypt the audio content itself end-to-end before sending.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access (Art. 15): Request a copy of your personal data.
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16): Correct inaccurate data.
  • Right to Erasure (Art. 17): Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Right to Restriction (Art. 18): Request restriction of processing.
  • Right to Data Portability (Art. 20): Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object (Art. 21): Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent (Art. 7(3)): Revoke consent for optional analytics at any time in Settings.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint (Art. 77): File a complaint with the supervisory authority in your EU country of residence.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at app.transcribe.it@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.
